How to use this with your PLC

Every card is a teaching practice your team can choose to focus on together this year. We filtered out student and structural factors (prior achievement, class size, home life) so you only see moves you control.

The number is Hattie's effect size. The hinge point is 0.40 — roughly a year's growth in a year. Above it is where a strategy accelerates learning. Sort, search, and filter to explore; tap "Focus on this" to shortlist up to two. Green tags mark concrete, PLC-friendly starting points.
