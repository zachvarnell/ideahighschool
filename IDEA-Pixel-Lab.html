Using IDEA Pixel Lab

This is a lightweight, local raster editor. Images never leave your browser; save your work frequently.

  • V Move the currently selected layer (image and mask together); arrow keys nudge by 1 px or Shift+arrow by 10 px.
  • B / E Brush and eraser. Change brush size, hardness, and opacity along the top.
  • W / C Click a color for contiguous Magic Wand or all-image Color Range selection. Adjust Tolerance, Feather and the source (active Layer or Merged).
  • Remove BG isolates solid-color backgrounds connected to the image edges. For photos with complex backgrounds use a mask and refine with brushes.
  • Brush presets include round, airbrush, marker, chalk, dry brush, spray, splatter and pixel. Use Effects for grain, texture, halftone, blur and warping.
  • PSD mockups: Open PSD imports many 8-bit RGB/Grayscale documents into raster layers, including available masks and blend modes. Use Replace to swap a layer's raster artwork inside its existing bounds. Smart objects, editable text, clipping groups and Photoshop layer effects are not recreated faithfully.
  • M / L Rectangle selection or freehand lasso. Painting affects only the selected area. Press Delete to clear selected pixels.
  • Mask: select an area with M or L, then click + Mask to hide everything outside the selection; without a selection, + Mask starts fully visible. Click the mask thumbnail, use B and choose Hide (black) or Reveal (white). Click the image thumbnail to paint pixels again.
  • Layers: use Up/Down to reorder, Copy to duplicate, choose a blend mode and change layer opacity. Double-click a layer name to rename it.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z; Redo: Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y; Deselect: Ctrl+D.
  • Save: Ctrl+S downloads a layered .json project; Open Project resumes editing. Export PNG/JPG creates a flattened image. Save frequently; offline tabs are not backed up.
  • Drag images onto the canvas or paste an image from your clipboard to add a new layer.

PSD import is best-effort and rasterized: no smart-object replacement workflow, PSD export, live text or exact Photoshop effects. Background removal is color-based, not AI. Keep original PSD files.
