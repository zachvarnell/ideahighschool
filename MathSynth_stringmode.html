Harmonic Mixer EquationDefault: first sine harmonic only.

Only nonzero-amplitude terms are shown (plus the PWM DC offset when applicable). If all amplitudes are zero, f(x) = 0. x is in radians.

ADSR times are in seconds. Sustain is a percentage. Filter envelope sweeps the cutoff upward from its base value. The oscilloscope displays the post-effects audio signal. The SHIFT key freezes the oscilloscope.

The A through ; keys work as white keys, and W through P as the black keys. The space bar plays the note in the harmonic mixer.
